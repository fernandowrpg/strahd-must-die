# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Journal compendium with the complete Strahd Must Die zine (14 chapters, 46 pages), styled after the original zine.
- *The Conspiracy of Ravens* Cards deck with all 54 Omen Cards.
- Roll tables: *The Mist Table (1d20)* and *Omen Cards*.
- *Gothic journal theme* client setting.
- Compatible with Foundry VTT v13 and v14.
- Errata applied to the zine text:
  - Ace of Spades is named *Destruction*, as in the deck legend. The card header in the PDF repeated *Knight*.
  - Jack of Diamonds is named *Elder God*, as on the card itself. The PDF legend listed *Beast*.
  - Fixed typos in the PDF: *Pyromancer*, *Shield*, *Transmutation*, *Shepherd's Crook*, *The Visitor*, *The Mist*, *Werewolf*, *Raising the Stakes*.
